## 1.2 Onboarding Survey  · 20 min

A 17-part student intake / baseline survey (1.2.1–1.2.17). Primarily data collection, minimal teaching content. Sections:
- **Welcome / consent** ("Thank you for joining IFE! Please be honest…").
- **Identity**: full name, email.
- **Entrepreneurial background**: prior interest in starting a business; prior entrepreneurial activity (yes / I think so / no); 2–3 sentence experience description; whether they know any business owners; 1–5 interest in starting a business/organization.
- **Skills self-evaluation (1–5)**: collaboration, research, comfort with technology, stress from mistakes, problem-solving, empathy, creativity, environmental awareness, communication, helping others, organization/detail, money management, job-market readiness, fear of failure, turning plans into action, community involvement.
- **Reflective short-answers**: how you learn/solve problems; a recent problem solved; a teamwork example; one weakness; one strength.
- **Learning style** (multi-select): Reading/Writing, Auditory, Visual, Kinesthetic, Social/Interpersonal, Other.
- **What you look forward to learning.**
- **Academic standing self-evaluation (1–5)**: school engagement, academic performance, confidence to graduate / attend college, whether school teaches practical/income skills, job confidence, career drive, sense of control over future, mentor support.
- **Academic short-answers**: what you'd change about school; biggest challenges re: college; dream job(s); 3 industries of interest; a weakness; a strength.
- **Fulfillment**: what's important to be truly happy/fulfilled.
- **Demographics (1.2.16)**: age, pronouns, school type, school name, household income level, first-generation college status, neighborhood zipcode, socioeconomic indicators (public/affordable housing, lunch voucher, SNAP/welfare, homelessness, parent unemployment, working outside school, family income provider, recent immigration, ESL), person-of-color identity, racial/ethnic identity (multi-select), accommodations/requests.
- **Program understanding & feedback (1.2.17)**: how well they understand the Cambio Labs Social Entrepreneurship Incubator program (1–5), difficulty of the form (1–5), open questions/feedback.
